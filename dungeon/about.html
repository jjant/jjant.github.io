01 / The field guide

Take a breath.
Then take your turn.

Not every treasure is worth the detour.
That’s where the adventure starts.

I.

Every floor asks something new

Explore rooms and corridors. Walls hide enemies; explored terrain stays grey outside your view. Save a potion; find the stairs. Each guardian must fall before its staircase opens.

II.

Read the warning

Warning marks lock the cells an enemy will attack after your next valid action. Step aside, or land a shove to cancel its intent. Aim at highlighted cells with the arrow keys; aiming and canceling don’t spend a turn.

III.

Make their move yours

Strikes and charges can hit other monsters too. Lure them into each other’s paths. After attacking, enemies need one action to recover: that’s your opening.

In the prototype Ready to combine

A little magic.
A lot of possibility.

Light the reeds. Douse a firebreak. Shove an enemy onto spikes or into a wall. Simple rules turn the room into part of your plan.

Unlock now. Open later. Throw a key into a locked chest: the key is used up, but the treasure can wait. Bump into the chest to open it when you’re ready.

Mind the blob. Keys and coins do no damage and won’t interrupt its attack. A glass potion still hurts and interrupts it.
