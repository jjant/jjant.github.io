01 / The field guide

Take a breath.
Then take your turn.

Not every treasure is worth the detour.
That’s where the adventure starts.

I.

Every floor asks something new

Take a detour for treasure, save a potion, find the stairs. Two guardians stand between you and the final exit. Each must fall before its staircase opens.

II.

Read the warning

Golden Xs lock the cells an enemy will attack after your next valid action. Step aside, or land a shove to cancel its intent. Aiming and canceling don’t spend a turn.

III.

Make their move yours

Strikes and charges can hit other monsters too. Lure them into each other’s paths. After attacking, enemies need one action to recover: that’s your opening.
